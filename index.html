A few things to know

Curiosity
is welcome.

A first session can come with questions. Here’s a little clarity before we connect.

Ask Ishha a question
Is this for me if I’m new to Tarot?

Absolutely. You don’t need any knowledge of the cards. Bring a question, an area of life you’d like to explore, or simply your curiosity. Ishha will guide the conversation.

Can I connect online?

Online sessions are available on enquiry. Message Ishha to confirm the format, your preferred time and current availability. If you’re in Chandrapur, you can also ask about in-person options.

What can I ask about?

You can explore relationships, career direction, personal growth or a period of change. Tarot is a spiritual tool for reflection and exploring possibilities; it doesn’t promise a certain outcome or replace qualified professional advice.

How long is a session, and what does it cost?

Duration and fees depend on the service and the session you choose. Ishha will share current details directly on WhatsApp, so you can decide before confirming an appointment.

How do I book my session?

Tap “Book a session” or “Connect on WhatsApp” to start a conversation with Ishha at +91 93710 36310. You can confirm the service, fees and time together. Opening WhatsApp prepares an enquiry; it doesn’t automatically send a message or confirm a booking.
